# Focusly

**Live Demo:** [https://focusly-mnw4-ten.vercel.app](https://focusly-mnw4-ten.vercel.app)

A minimalist workstation designed for deep focus and productivity.

## Features

* 52/17 Rule: Scientific focus and recovery sessions based on high-performance research.
* Task Management: A minimalist todo list to track your daily objectives.
* Ambient Sounds: A curated selection of high-rated focus sounds for an immersive environment( 40 hz binaural beats included,rain,wind,white noise,brown noise,beach waves,forest).
* Aesthetic Interface: A frosted-glass nature HUD designed to minimize distractions.

## Getting Started

1. Install dependencies:
   npm install

2. Run the development server:
   npm run dev

3. Open the application in your browser:
   http://localhost:5173

## The Science of 52/17

Focusly is built on the 52/17 productivity rule, a method validated by researchers for maintaining high levels of concentration throughout the day.

### Benefits of the 52/17 Method

* Cognitive Reset: Research from the University of Cambridge highlights that 17-minute breaks prevent "directed attention fatigue," allowing your brain to fully recover before the next focus block.
* Flow State Optimization: A 52-minute focus period is the ideal length for entering a deep state of flow—reaching peak productivity without the burnout associated with longer, unbroken work hours.
* Digital Well-being: Switching off from screens during the 17-minute recovery period reduces eye strain and mental clutter, leading to better long-term performance.

For more information, you can view the research summary and discussion by University of Cambridge researcher Dr. Olivia Remes here:
[How to boost productivity and wellbeing with the 52/17 rule](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/university-of-cambridge_university-of-cambridge-productivity-activity-7384943972936486912-ioxv)
